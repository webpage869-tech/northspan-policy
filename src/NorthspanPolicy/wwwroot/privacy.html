Privacy Notice

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This notice explains what information Northspan Ltd collects through our AI policy products, how we use it, and how we protect it. Contact us about privacy at info@northspanltd.com.

We do not see or store your card details. Payments are handled by our payment provider under its own privacy terms.

Your uploaded policy

We treat any policy you upload as confidential.

  • It is used only to check it and to produce what you asked for
  • It is not saved after the check, and we do not keep copies
  • It is never sold, shared for marketing, or used to train any AI model
  • Northspan staff do not read it unless you ask us for support with your order

Please remove names, account numbers and other personal details from your policy before uploading. A policy document rarely needs them.

Use of AI

We use an artificial intelligence service provided by Anthropic (the Claude API) to read uploaded policies and generate reports and updated policies. The text is sent securely from our server to Anthropic for processing. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, prompts and responses are not retained after processing by default and are not used to train its models without permission. Content that Anthropic's systems flag for possible misuse may be kept for safety review.

Where your data is stored

Our website and files are hosted on servers provided by Hetzner Online in the United States. AI processing takes place on Anthropic's systems, which may be outside your country.

Marketing

We will only send you marketing emails if you tick the box to agree. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your information, unless we must keep it for legal or tax reasons
  • Stop sending you marketing

Email info@northspanltd.com and we will respond within 30 days. You may also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), limit access to customer data to those who need it, and keep secret keys out of our code. No system is completely secure, and we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your information.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. The latest version is always on this page, with its date.
